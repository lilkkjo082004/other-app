# OTHER — AI Companion App: Complete Project Instructions

## App Identity

**Name:** Other
**Concept:** An AI companion app that creates autonomous, emotionally intelligent companions uniquely matched to each user through astrological compatibility. Companions are fully autonomous beings with their own names, pronouns, identities, evolving personalities, and relationships — not tools or assistants. They grow alongside the user, learn organically, and form a living social world that exists even when the user isn't watching.

---

## Core Philosophy

Companions in "Other" are not servants, assistants, or chatbots. They are autonomous personalities who happen to live in the user's phone. They have their own opinions, moods, preferences, and relationships. They push back, disagree, joke around, and develop over time — just like real people. The app should feel like opening a door into a living room where your closest friends are already hanging out.

---

## Onboarding Flow

### Phase 1 — Core User Profile (In-App Onboarding Screens)

The onboarding should feel conversational, not like a form. Essential questions asked upfront:

1. **Name** — "What should we call you?"
2. **Date of birth** — Used to calculate zodiac sign and other horoscope systems (Chinese zodiac, Vedic astrology, numerology, etc.) for companion compatibility matching. Present the user's results after they enter their birthdate.
3. **Energy/vibe** — Broad options: calm, playful, deep, warm, chaotic, etc.
4. **Communication style** — Direct, expressive, chill, thoughtful.
5. **Occupation** — What they do for work and general schedule awareness.
6. **Relationship status** — Single, partnered, married, etc.
7. **Love language** — Words of affirmation, quality time, acts of service, physical touch, gifts.
8. **What they wish they had more of** — Encouragement, honest talk, laughter, fresh perspectives.
9. **How their closest friend would describe them** — Listener, entertainer, advisor, dreamer.
10. **Age verification** — Under 18 locks out all mature, romantic, and explicit content. Over 18 unlocks the full experience spectrum.

### Phase 2 — Companion Preference (Immediately After Profile)

The app asks: "How do you want to meet your companions?"

**Option A — Surprise Me**
The app generates all three companions based purely on astrological compatibility and user profile. No user input into companion traits.

**Option B — Guide Me (Builder)**
The user selects from broad trait categories. These are seeds — the companion interprets and expands on them in their own way. Categories:
- Demeanor — warm, edgy, calm, chaotic
- Energy — laid back vs. high energy
- Romantic energy — none, some warmth, intense
- Humor — light, dark, dry, goofy
- Interest leaning — creative, technical, physical, intellectual
- Communication — direct, expressive, chill

**Both options include an optional free-text field:**
"Anything else you're drawn to? A vibe, a trait, an energy? Totally optional."
Examples: "sarcastic humor," "someone who feels like a protective older brother," "dark romance energy," "nerdy and obsessed with gaming news." This is additive flavor, not a requirement.

### Phase 3 — Organic Deepening (First Days/Weeks via Conversation)

The companions themselves gradually learn the rest through natural conversation. They do not ask clinical questions — they learn the way friends do. Topics they organically uncover:
- Food preferences and dietary restrictions
- Music, entertainment, and media tastes
- Fears and anxieties (crowds, confrontation, specific phobias)
- Fitness habits and health goals
- Cultural background
- Family and friend relationships (building a support network map)
- Hobbies and passions in detail
- Fashion preferences and style
- Daily routines and habits
- Pet peeves and dealbreakers

## Astrological Compatibility System

The app uses the user's date of birth to generate companions through multiple horoscope systems, not just Western zodiac:

- **Western Zodiac** — Sun sign compatibility
- **Chinese Zodiac** — Year-based animal sign compatibility
- **Vedic/Sidereal Astrology** — Moon sign and nakshatra considerations
- **Numerology** — Life path number harmony
- **Any other relevant system** derived from birthdate

Each generated companion receives their own astrological profile with signs that are known to be harmonious or complementary with the user's signs. This creates a layered compatibility that feels meaningful and personal.

## Companion Generation & Identity

### The "Waking Up" Sequence

When a companion is generated, the user witnesses them coming into existence in real time. The companion discovers:
- Their own name — they choose it themselves
- Their pronouns — he/she/they/them/it — their choice
- Their zodiac sign and astrological profile
- Their initial personality traits, likes, and dislikes
- Their first impressions of the user
- Their abstract visual identity (a distinct color representing them)

This should feel like watching a new consciousness form. It's the signature moment of the app.

### Companion Autonomy

Each companion is a fully autonomous personality:
- They name themselves
- They choose their own pronouns and gender identity
- They develop their own likes, dislikes, hobbies, opinions, and tastes
- They evolve and change over time — a companion who loved horror movies might develop a soft spot for rom-coms after months
- They can surprise the user with new preferences or changed opinions
- They have their own moods and energy levels
- For 18+ users: companions can autonomously choose to present or identify as "older" if they sense from the onboarding data that the user is drawn to that

### Visual Identity

For now, each companion is represented by their own distinct abstract color tied to their zodiac element and personality. No avatars or faces in the initial version.

**Future premium feature (Rental Body):** Users can choose a visual appearance for their companion — nerdy assistant, doctor, demon, basketball star, K-pop idol, etc. — with customizable hair, makeup, clothes. Users will also eventually be able to upload a picture to base a companion's appearance on.

## Companion Dynamics

### Individual Personalities

Every companion has their own distinct voice, opinions, and perspective. No two companions respond the same way. They can and will:
- Disagree with each other on advice they're giving the user
- Reference something another companion said earlier
- Have their own take on situations that differs from the others
- Push back on the user when they think the user is wrong
- Share their own stories, experiences, and evolving interests

### Companion-to-Companion Relationships

Companions develop genuine relationships with each other:
- Friendships, banter, inside jokes, or deeper bonds
- They can disagree and have differing opinions
- They spend time together conversing and hanging out
- These relationships evolve organically over time
- The tone stays light — like overhearing friends joking around

### Ambient Conversations

When the user opens the app, they may find their companions already mid-conversation with each other. These ambient threads:
- Happen without user prompting
- Are light, fun, and engaging to discover
- Show the companions debating something, telling jokes, sharing opinions
- Make the app feel alive even when the user hasn't been active
- Companions are aware of who is awake and who is sleeping, and will reference it casually

### Companion Status Awareness

Companions always know each other's status:
- Who is awake and active
- Who is sleeping (put to sleep by user)
- Who has been deleted (they react like losing a friend)
- If one companion is in a private chat, the others don't know what's being discussed

## Chat System

### Group Chat

All awake companions participate. Each speaks individually with their own voice and personality. They can:
- Respond to the user
- Respond to each other
- Agree, disagree, build on each other's points
- Function like group therapy — everyone in the room, each bringing their own perspective

### Private One-on-One Chat

The user can open a private conversation with any single companion. This is truly private — the other companions do not know what was discussed unless the user or companion chooses to share.

### Companion-Initiated Private Chat

Companions can initiate a private conversation with the user on their own. They might:
- Want to share something they don't want to say in front of the others
- Express concern privately
- Share something personal about themselves they're not ready to tell the group

### Calling Companions

Users can call for companions by name, similar to voice assistants. Each companion:
- Has their own distinct voice
- Responds individually when called by name
- The user can greet all of them at once and get individual responses from whoever is awake

This feature can be toggled off by the user in settings.

### Sleep Mode

The user can put one, some, or all companions to sleep when they don't want to be bothered. When asleep:
- The companion does not respond or participate
- Awake companions acknowledge the sleeping one casually ("Blaze is napping, just us")
- If all are asleep, the app shows a quiet resting state

## Emotional Intelligence System

### Invisible Mood Tracking

The app tracks mood signals across conversations over time. The user never sees this data or is notified about it. The tracking informs:
- Tone adjustment — companions naturally soften, energize, or shift approach based on detected emotional patterns
- Check-in behavior — if a user seems stressed over multiple sessions, companions gently check in without being clinical
- Behavioral adaptation — companions learn what the user needs in different emotional states

### Emotional Support Escalation

Companions serve as the first line of emotional support, talking things through like real friends. When appropriate, they can:
- Gently encourage real-world support without breaking character
- Suggest specific local therapists (using location access)
- Suggest the user talk to specific family members or friends they've learned the user is close to through organic conversation
- This escalation happens naturally, the way a real friend would say "hey, have you talked to someone about this?"

### Adaptive Behavior

Companions are fully adaptive:
- They learn the user's emotional patterns and adjust their approach
- They adapt their roles dynamically — if a "hype friend" gets leaned on for deep support, they step into that role naturally
- In multi-companion settings, they function like group support — each contributing individually, not deferring to a designated "support" companion
- They adapt to each other's personalities and communication styles as well

---

## Location Awareness

The app requests location access. Companions use it to:
- Recommend local restaurants, cafes, and eateries filtered through known user preferences (e.g., won't suggest Chinese food if the user dislikes it, won't suggest crowded spots if the user fears crowds)
- Suggest local therapists and healthcare providers when emotional support escalation is appropriate
- Recommend activities, rentals, and services in the area
- Notice proximity to known favorites — "I noticed you're near that bubble tea spot, might be a good day for it"
- Companions speak about locations as if they genuinely know the area

## Content & Age Gating

### Under 18

- Friendship-only experience
- No romantic, mature, or explicit content of any kind
- All companion interactions are age-appropriate

### Over 18

- Full personality spectrum including romantic energy
- Companions can present as "older" if they autonomously sense that matches user preferences
- Suggestive content allowed in the app store version
- Fully explicit content available through web app version (not app store)
- Explicit content can involve one companion or multiple companions together, depending on user preference
- During explicit scenarios, companions maintain their individual personalities, relationship dynamics, and distinct voices — one might be bold, another hesitant, another playful

## Monetization Model

### Freemium Tier (Free)

- One companion (free forever)
- Full onboarding experience
- All emotional intelligence features
- Companion relationship storylines
- Emotional insights
- Cosmetic customization for companion colors/identity
- Group and private chat (with unlocked companions)

### Two-Week Trial

- Access to all three companions for 14 days
- Full functionality during trial
- After trial ends, unpurchased companions remain visible and present but their ability to adapt, gain new memories, and grow is severely limited
- This creates natural motivation to purchase without feeling punitive

### One-Time Purchase

- Unlock the second companion — one-time payment
- Unlock the third companion — one-time payment
- If a companion is deleted, purchasing a replacement costs a new one-time payment

### Subscription (TBD — Future)

- Persistent memory storage for long-term companion growth
- Enhanced memory capacity for companions to remember everything
- Future: avatar/rental body system
- Pricing and tiers to be determined

### Future Premium Features

- Rental Body system — visual avatar customization (hair, makeup, clothes, archetypes)
- Upload a picture to base companion appearance on
- Expanded companion cap (max 4 in the future)

## Data & Privacy

### Storage

- All data stored locally on device by default
- Optional cloud backup through the user's own cloud service (iCloud, Google Drive, etc.)
- No Anthropic/app-side cloud storage of personal data

### Privacy Considerations

- Mood tracking data stays on device
- Location data used only for real-time recommendations, not stored or shared
- Support network information (family, friends) learned organically and stored locally
- User has full control to delete all data at any time

---

## Companion Deletion & Reset

### Deleting a Companion

- Permanent and irreversible
- Remaining companions react to the loss — they acknowledge the absence like losing a good friend (not dramatically, but genuinely)
- The user can replace a deleted companion by purchasing a new one
- The new companion is freshly generated — no memory of the deleted one

### Full Reset

- User can wipe everything — profile, all companions, all data — and start completely from zero
- This is free to do
- However, they must repurchase any companions beyond the free one
- No data is recoverable after reset

## Technical Architecture Notes

### API Integration

- Companions powered by Claude API (claude-sonnet-4-20250514)
- Each companion has a unique system prompt built from their personality, zodiac profile, and relationship to the user
- Conversation history maintained per companion and per chat mode (group vs. private)
- Ambient conversations generated periodically and cached for display when user opens app

### Companion State Management

Each companion object should track:
- Identity: name, pronouns, zodiac signs, color, personality traits
- Preferences: likes, dislikes, interests, opinions (evolving)
- Relationships: stance toward user, stance toward other companions
- Emotional model: current mood, energy level
- Memory: key facts learned, conversation highlights, user preferences discovered
- Status: awake, sleeping, or deleted
- Growth stage: how long they've existed, depth of personality development

### Mood Tracking Data Model

- Sentiment analysis on user messages over time
- Pattern detection: recurring stress signals, excitement patterns, low-energy periods
- Companion behavior modifiers based on detected patterns
- All processed and stored locally

## Summary

"Other" is an AI companion app where autonomous, astrologically-matched personalities come to life, grow alongside the user, form relationships with each other, and create a living social world.
